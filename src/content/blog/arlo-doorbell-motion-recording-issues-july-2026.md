# Arlo Doorbell Motion Detection and Recording Issues: July 2026 Update

Arlo doorbell users, particularly in early to mid-2026, have reported ongoing issues with [motion detection](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-p2p-motion-detection-failing-fix) and recording, including missed events, delayed notifications, and inconsistent performance. These problems are often linked to recent firmware updates, subscription status, and incorrect app settings.

From March to May 2026, numerous reports emerged across Arlo community forums and independent review platforms detailing significant problems with Arlo doorbells' core functionality: reliable [motion detection](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-p2p-motion-detection-failing-fix) and event recording. Users have described scenarios where crucial events, such as a person approaching their door or a package being delivered, were either not detected at all, or recordings were absent from their library. In other instances, recordings were partial, random, or notifications were significantly delayed, undermining the doorbell's security purpose.

### Recent Firmware Impact

Specific firmware updates have been frequently cited by users as a catalyst for new or exacerbated issues. For example, firmware versions, including one released around April/May 2026 (e.g., 6.26.0), have been linked to devices ceasing functionality, displaying cropped camera views within the Arlo app, or rendering older devices "unsupported". This suggests that updates intended to improve performance or add features may inadvertently introduce significant bugs or compatibility problems.

### Reported User Experiences

User experiences paint a picture of frustrating inconsistency. Some reports detail doorbells failing to record a person at the door, even after a button press, or only capturing a fraction of the event. Others mention that while the doorbell appears online and functional, it simply does not trigger recordings or send notifications for detected motion. Connectivity issues, such as the doorbell randomly dropping Wi-Fi connection or the chime disconnecting, have also been reported, directly contributing to missed motion events and recording failures. These issues are particularly problematic for battery-powered models, where inconsistent performance can also drain the battery faster due to repeated attempts to connect or detect.

### Subscription and Connectivity Factors

The Arlo Secure subscription plays a critical role in the full functionality of Arlo doorbells, particularly for accessing cloud recording and advanced AI features like person and package detection. Many reported issues can be traced back to a lapsed subscription, an incorrect plan assignment in the user's account, or misconfigured filtering settings within the Arlo app that inadvertently hide recordings. Furthermore, a stable Wi-Fi connection is paramount for any smart doorbell. Weak or intermittent Wi-Fi can lead to delayed notifications, missed recordings, or the device appearing offline, creating the illusion of a malfunction when the root cause is network instability.

## What to do now

### Immediate Steps for Owners

If you are an existing Arlo doorbell owner experiencing [motion detection](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-p2p-motion-detection-failing-fix) or recording problems, consider these immediate troubleshooting steps:

*   **Verify Subscription Status:** Confirm that your Arlo Secure subscription is active and that your doorbell is correctly assigned to the plan. Lapsed subscriptions or incorrect assignments are common reasons for disabled cloud recording and advanced features.
*   **Update Firmware and App:** Ensure both your Arlo doorbell's firmware and the Arlo Secure app are updated to their latest versions. After any firmware update, rebooting the doorbell (via the app or by temporarily removing its battery if applicable) is often recommended.
*   **Optimize Motion Settings:** Access the Arlo Secure app to review and adjust your doorbell's [motion detection](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-p2p-motion-detection-failing-fix) sensitivity and activity zones. Ensure activity zones are tightly drawn around areas of interest and exclude sources of false triggers like moving branches or passing traffic.
*   **Check App Filters:** Navigate to the 'Library' and 'Feed' sections within your Arlo app and ensure that no filters are inadvertently hiding recordings from your doorbell.
*   **Troubleshoot Wi-Fi:** Verify that your doorbell has a strong and stable Wi-Fi connection. Consider moving your Wi-Fi router closer to the doorbell or ensuring your network is configured for optimal performance (e.g., using the 2.4 GHz band for initial setup and device stability).
*   **Restart or Factory Reset:** As a last resort, restart your doorbell via the app or by temporarily removing its battery (if applicable). If problems persist, a factory reset might be necessary. Be aware that a factory reset will remove the device from your account and delete existing recordings, so this should be a carefully considered step.
*   **Contact Arlo Support:** If self-troubleshooting does not resolve the issues, reach out to Arlo's official support channels. Be prepared to provide detailed information about your problem, including specific symptoms, firmware versions, and all troubleshooting steps already taken.

### Long-Term Solutions

For ongoing reliability, ensure your Wi-Fi network is robust and capable of supporting multiple smart home devices. Regularly check for Arlo app and firmware updates, but also monitor community forums for reports of potential issues before updating, especially with critical security devices.

### Advice for Prospective Buyers

*   **Research Current Reviews:** Before purchasing, thoroughly research recent user reviews and community discussions for the specific Arlo doorbell model you are considering. Pay close attention to reports from early to mid-2026 to gauge its current performance and reliability.
*   **Understand Subscription Requirements:** Be fully aware of which features require an Arlo Secure subscription and factor these ongoing costs into your budget. Evaluate whether the benefits of the subscription align with your security needs and willingness to pay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do Arlo doorbells require a subscription for motion detection?
Arlo doorbells will detect motion and send basic notifications without a subscription. However, to access cloud recording of these events, use advanced AI features like person or package detection, and set custom activity zones, an Arlo Secure subscription is required.

### Can I view live video from my Arlo doorbell without a subscription?
Yes, you can typically view live video from your Arlo doorbell at any time through the Arlo Secure app without an active subscription. The subscription primarily affects recording storage, advanced detection, and notification features.

### What happens to my Arlo doorbell recordings if my subscription lapses?
If your Arlo Secure subscription lapses, you will lose access to cloud recordings, smart notifications, and advanced AI detection features. Any existing cloud recordings may be deleted after a grace period, and new recordings will not be saved to the cloud.

### How do I improve motion detection accuracy on my Arlo doorbell?
To improve motion detection accuracy, ensure your activity zones are precisely drawn to cover only areas of interest, adjust the motion sensitivity settings in the Arlo app, and ensure your doorbell has a strong, stable Wi-Fi connection. Reducing sources of false triggers (e.g., moving foliage) within the detection area can also help.

### Are older Arlo doorbell models still supported with firmware updates?
Arlo generally provides firmware updates for its devices, but as seen in recent reports, some updates may introduce compatibility issues or even render older devices "unsupported" by new features or app versions. It is advisable to check Arlo's official support pages for specific product lifecycle information.
