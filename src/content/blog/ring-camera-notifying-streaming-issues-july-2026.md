While the [official Ring Status page](https://status.ring.com/) and third-party monitors like StatusGator and IsDown indicate that all systems—including Video on Demand, mobile apps, and device health—are fully operational, community forums tell a different story.

### Official Status vs. User Reports
Despite the "all clear" from Ring’s backend monitors, localized clusters of users on the [Ring Community forum](https://community.ring.com/) and Reddit have reported significant hurdles with Live View connectivity and push notifications. These reports are not centralized to one region, suggesting that the issue may be tied to specific hardware configurations or recent software deployments rather than a global server outage.

Specific complaints highlight that Floodlight cameras, in particular, have struggled with push notifications following an iOS app update released in late June 2026. Additionally, users who recently migrated to the "Ring Home Pro" plan (the 2026 successor to Protect tiers) have noted that their previously reliable [motion alerts](https://quvii.com/blog/ring-camera-motion-alerts-not-working-july-2026) have become intermittent or stopped altogether.

### Impact of Recent AI Features and App Updates
The primary catalyst for these notification changes appears to be the rollout of "Unusual Event Alerts" and "Active Warnings," which Ring debuted at [CES 2026](https://www.ring.com/news) and began deploying in January. These AI-powered features are designed to "learn" the rhythms of a household—ignoring the daily mail delivery but alerting for a person loitering at 3:00 AM.

While intended to reduce notification fatigue, some users report that the AI is over-filtering events. In some cases, the system classifies actual movement as "normal activity," resulting in no notification being sent to the user’s phone. Furthermore, the "Active Warnings" feature, which allows the camera to play an automated voice message when it detects a person, has been linked to momentary streaming lag as the device prioritizes the audio processing over the initial Live View handshake.

## What to do now

![What to do now](/img/ring-camera-notifying-streaming-issues-july-2026/inline-4.webp)


If your Ring camera is currently failing to notify or stream, follow this systematic approach to restoration.

### Immediate Troubleshooting Steps
1.  **Audit the Ring App Settings:** Ensure that "[Motion Alerts](https://quvii.com/blog/ring-camera-motion-alerts-not-working-july-2026)" and "Ring Alerts" are toggled ON. Check the "Global Snooze" feature to ensure notifications haven't been accidentally silenced for the entire account.
2.  **Review AI Filtering:** If you are on a Pro plan, go to "Smart Alerts" and adjust the sensitivity of "Unusual Event Alerts." Setting this to "More Frequent" may resolve issues where the AI is being too restrictive.
3.  **Check Mobile Permissions:** On iOS and Android, ensure the Ring app has permission to "Ignore Do Not Disturb." In July 2026, many "missing notification" issues were traced back to the phone's OS-level "Focus Modes" rather than the Ring app itself.
4.  **Signal Strength (RSSI):** Navigate to "Device Health" and check the RSSI value. If it is higher than -60, your Wi-Fi signal is likely the culprit for failed streaming.
5.  **Reboot the Network:** A simple power cycle of your router can often clear the DNS cache issues that prevent the camera from establishing a secure "handshake" with Ring's servers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my Ring camera online but not sending notifications?
This is often due to the "Smart Alerts" or "Unusual Event" AI settings introduced in 2026. If the AI deems a movement "typical" for your environment, it may record the event without sending a push notification. Adjust your "Smart Alert" settings to "All Motion" to verify if the hardware is still detecting activity.

### How do I fix the "Failed to Connect" error when trying to Live View?
This error is usually caused by an upload speed bottleneck or a high RSSI (signal interference). Ensure your router's 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands are not congested. If you use a VPN on your mobile device, try disabling it, as VPNs frequently interfere with Ring's encrypted streaming protocols.

### Did Ring change their privacy policy regarding police access in 2026?
Yes, the June 5, 2026, update maintains that while Ring no longer facilitates the "Request for Assistance" tool in the Neighbors app, they reserve the right to provide footage to law enforcement in "emergency situations" where there is an imminent threat of death or serious physical injury, even without a warrant.

### Is there a way to store Ring footage locally to avoid subscription fees?
As of July 2026, Ring does not offer a native "subscription-free" local storage option for its standard cameras. The "Ring Edge" feature allows for local processing and storage on a Ring Alarm Pro base station with a microSD card, but this still requires an active Ring Home subscription to view the footage in the app.
