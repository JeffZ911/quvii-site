## What to do now

![What to do now](/img/reolink-camera-chime-motion-alerts-guide-2026/inline-4.webp)


To get the most out of your Reolink security system, proactive management of settings and regular updates are key. Regularly check for and install the latest firmware updates via the Reolink App, Client, or NVR to ensure access to the newest features, security enhancements, and performance improvements.

Optimize motion detection settings by adjusting sensitivity (typically a slider from 1-50 or 1-100) based on your environment and desired alert frequency to prevent missed events or excessive notifications. Define precise detection zones to exclude areas prone to false alarms, such as public sidewalks or swaying foliage, focusing the camera's attention on critical areas. Configure object size filters for smart detection (person, vehicle, pet) to ensure the camera only triggers alerts for relevant subjects, further reducing unwanted notifications. Set up customized alert schedules to activate specific detection types and notification methods (e.g., push, email, siren) during different times of the day or week, aligning with your security needs.

For Reolink doorbells, ensure proper chime pairing (if not pre-paired) and adjust the chime volume (five levels available) and select from the ten available ringtones via the Reolink App, Client, NVR, or web browser. If using a Reolink battery doorbell with an existing wired chime, verify compatibility and ensure your doorbell transformer meets the specified voltage (8-24 VAC) and current (5-40VA) requirements for proper operation. Explore advanced alert options, including NVR buzzer sounds and camera sirens, to create a multi-layered alert system that responds effectively to detected motion. Review Reolink's privacy policy and utilize privacy mask features to blur out sensitive areas within the camera's field of view, enhancing personal privacy.

### Updating Your Reolink Firmware

Keeping your Reolink devices updated is crucial for both security and functionality. Firmware updates often introduce new features, improve detection algorithms, and patch security vulnerabilities. You can typically check for and install updates directly through the Reolink App on your smartphone, the Reolink Client software on your computer, or through the web interface if your camera supports it. Always ensure a stable internet connection and sufficient power before initiating a firmware update to prevent interruptions.

### Optimizing Motion Detection for Fewer False Alarms

To minimize false alarms, carefully configure your camera's motion detection settings. Start by adjusting the sensitivity level; a lower sensitivity can reduce triggers from minor movements like leaves or small animals, while a higher setting ensures even subtle activity is caught. Next, define specific motion detection zones, focusing on critical areas and excluding busy public spaces or areas with constant, irrelevant motion. Utilize the smart detection features (person, vehicle, pet detection) to filter out unwanted alerts, ensuring you only receive notifications for significant events.

### Configuring Your Reolink Doorbell Chime

Reolink doorbells offer versatile chime options. If you have a dedicated Reolink wireless chime, ensure it is properly paired with your doorbell camera, which can usually be done through the Reolink App. Within the app, you can customize the chime's volume across five levels and choose from ten different ringtones to suit your preference. For those with existing wired mechanical or digital chimes, verify that your doorbell transformer provides the necessary 8-24 VAC (Volts alternating current) and 5-40VA (Volt-amperes) power to ensure proper integration and functionality.

### Leveraging Advanced Alert Options

Beyond standard push notifications, Reolink provides several advanced alert options to enhance your security response. Many cameras feature built-in sirens that can be triggered automatically upon detection or manually through the app, serving as a deterrent. If your camera is connected to a Reolink NVR, you can configure the NVR to emit a buzzer sound when motion is detected, providing an audible alert even if your phone is not nearby. Email alerts with snapshots or video clips can also be set up for a comprehensive notification system.

### Reviewing Privacy Settings and Detection Zones

Regularly reviewing your camera's privacy settings and detection zones is vital for maintaining personal privacy and effective monitoring. Utilize privacy mask features to block out specific areas within the camera's field of view that you do not wish to record, such as a neighbor's window or public property. Ensure your detection zones are precisely drawn to focus on areas relevant to your security, avoiding unnecessary surveillance of public spaces. This balance helps ensure you capture critical events while respecting privacy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I stop getting so many false alarms from my Reolink camera?
To reduce false alarms, access your camera's settings in the Reolink App or Client. Adjust the motion detection sensitivity to a lower level, define specific detection zones to exclude areas with irrelevant movement (like trees or public sidewalks), and enable smart detection filters for persons, vehicles, or pets to ignore other types of motion.

### Can I use my existing wired doorbell chime with a Reolink video doorbell?
Yes, many Reolink wired video doorbells are compatible with existing mechanical or digital chimes. You will need to ensure your doorbell transformer provides power within the specified range, typically 8-24 VAC and 5-40VA. The Reolink App will guide you through the setup and chime type selection.

### Do Reolink cameras require a subscription for motion alerts?
No, Reolink cameras generally do not require a subscription for basic motion alerts or advanced AI-powered smart detection (person, vehicle, package). These features are typically processed on-device, allowing for subscription-free alerts and local video storage via microSD card or NVR.

### How do I adjust the volume or change the ringtone of my Reolink chime?
For dedicated Reolink wireless chimes, you can adjust the volume (usually five levels) and select from ten different ringtones directly through the Reolink App. Navigate to your doorbell's settings and look for the "Chime Settings" or similar option to customize these preferences.

### What is the Reolink AI Box and how does it help with motion detection?
The Reolink AI Box is an independent device designed to bring advanced local AI processing capabilities (like person/vehicle detection) to older Reolink cameras that do not have these features built-in. It acts as a hub, processing video streams locally to generate more intelligent and accurate motion alerts without needing cloud subscriptions.
