# Eufy Security Motion Push Notifications: Understanding Recent Failures (July 2026)

Eufy security camera users have reported recurring issues with motion [push notifications](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-push-notifications-broken-haos-2026-6-4), often linked to recent firmware updates for HomeBase 3 and various camera models (February-May 2026) and app-related glitches, which can lead to delayed or missed alerts in July 2026. While Eufy provides extensive troubleshooting, some problems stem from software bugs acknowledged by the company.

Multiple user reports and community discussions indicate a pattern of unreliable motion [push notifications](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-push-notifications-broken-haos-2026-6-4) and related system instabilities for Eufy security cameras and HomeBase devices in the months leading up to July 2026. Eufy users have frequently taken to online forums, including Reddit's r/EufyCam community, to share experiences of delayed, inconsistent, or altogether absent [motion alerts](https://quvii.com/blog/reolink-camera-chime-motion-alerts-guide-2026) from their security systems. These discussions often detail scenarios where motion events are recorded by the camera, but the corresponding [push notification](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-security-notification-failure-june-2026) to the user's smartphone either arrives significantly late or fails to appear at all, undermining the real-time alert function critical for security.

### Firmware Update Instabilities (February-May 2026)

Specific firmware updates, particularly for HomeBase 3 (versions 3.8.2.0 and 3.8.2.8) and certain camera models, released between February and May 2026, have been cited as causes for issues like cameras going offline, excessive battery drain, and inconsistent [motion detection](https://quvii.com/blog/ring-camera-motion-streaming-issues-july-2026). For instance, a February 2026 update for some models was reported to cause continuous recording to stop at specific times daily. Users also reported significant network instability with HomeBase 3 following certain updates.

In April 2026, many users experienced a widespread failure where multiple Eufy devices stopped reliably detecting motion around April 11-12, affecting various camera types and doorbells simultaneously. This broad impact suggested a systemic issue, likely linked to a server-side change or a widely distributed firmware update. A May 2026 HomeBase 3 update (v.3.8.2.0) further exacerbated problems for some, causing loading loops and the loss of settings and events, often linked to a new video encoding or restructuring prompt within the app. Eufy support has, in some cases, acknowledged these issues as firmware-related and has been working on fixes, though a complete resolution for all users is not always immediate.

### App-Related Notification Glitches

Beyond firmware, Eufy's mobile application itself can contribute to notification inconsistencies. Users frequently report that even with correct camera and HomeBase settings, the Eufy Security app might fail to process or deliver [push notifications](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-push-notifications-broken-haos-2026-6-4) reliably due to background app restrictions, power optimization settings on smartphones, or general app bugs. Issues like notifications being delayed by several minutes or not appearing until the app is manually opened are common complaints, indicating a potential disconnect between the camera's detection, the HomeBase's processing, and the app's notification delivery mechanism.

### Specific Camera and HomeBase 3 Impacts

The HomeBase 3, intended as a central hub for enhanced AI and local storage, has been a focal point for many of the reported issues. Firmware updates for HomeBase 3 have been directly linked to cameras going offline, increased battery drain for connected devices, and general system unreliability. Specific camera models like the S330 (eufyCam 3), E330, and S340 ([SoloCam S340](https://quvii.com/learn/reolink-argus-4-pro-vs-eufy-solocam-s340)) have also been mentioned in user reports as experiencing notification failures and other [performance issues](https://quvii.com/learn/eufy-app-performance-issues-june-2026-20260630) following recent updates. The integrated nature of the Eufy ecosystem means that problems with the HomeBase can propagate, affecting the performance and notification reliability of all connected cameras.

## What to do now

![What to do now](/img/eufy-motion-push-notifications-failing-july-2026/inline-4.webp)


If you are an existing Eufy owner experiencing [motion push notification](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-security-notification-failure-june-2026) failures, several steps can be taken to troubleshoot the issue.

### Immediate Troubleshooting Steps

1.  **Check Phone Settings:** Verify that [push notifications](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-push-notifications-broken-haos-2026-6-4) are enabled for the [Eufy app](https://quvii.com/learn/eufy-app-performance-issues-june-2026-20260630) in your phone's system settings. Ensure battery optimization is set to 'unrestricted' or 'not optimized' for the Eufy app to allow it to run continuously in the background. Also, confirm that the app is permitted to use data in the background.
2.  **Review Eufy App Settings:** Open the Eufy Security app and confirm that [motion detection](https://quvii.com/blog/ring-camera-motion-streaming-issues-july-2026) is enabled for each camera experiencing issues. Ensure that [push notifications](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-push-notifications-broken-haos-2026-6-4) are active within your selected security mode (e.g., Home, Away). Navigate to the individual camera settings to verify that "[Push Notification](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-security-notification-failure-june-2026)" is toggled on for motion detection alerts.
3.  **Inspect [Motion Detection](https://quvii.com/blog/ring-camera-motion-streaming-issues-july-2026) Zones and Sensitivity:** Ensure activity zones are correctly defined and sensitivity levels are appropriate for your environment. Incorrect settings can lead to missed events or excessive false alarms, which can then desensitize users to notifications.
4.  **Reboot Devices:** Perform a power cycle on your Eufy cameras, HomeBase, and Wi-Fi router. Unplug each device, wait for about 30 seconds, and then plug them back in. This can resolve temporary connectivity glitches and refresh network connections.
5.  **Check for Firmware/App Updates:** Ensure your Eufy app and all Eufy devices have the latest firmware. Eufy typically rolls out updates gradually, so manual checks might be necessary. Within the Eufy app, go to `Side Menu > My Devices > HomeBase > Device Settings > About Device` to check for firmware updates. For cameras, select the individual camera and look for a similar "About Device" or "Firmware Update" option.

## Decision Framework: Choosing a Security Camera System

Navigating the security camera market requires understanding your specific needs. Here's a decision framework to guide your purchase:

*   **If you are a Renter prioritizing privacy and no monthly fees:** Prioritize cameras with robust local storage options (microSD card or a local hub) and a strong privacy policy. Look for brands like Eufy (with local HomeBase storage), Reolink, or Tapo that offer comprehensive features without mandatory subscriptions.
*   **If you are a Homeowner seeking comprehensive coverage with advanced AI:** Consider systems that integrate multiple cameras with a central hub, offering AI detection (person, vehicle, pet) and local storage. Eufy HomeBase 3 systems are designed for this, but also evaluate professional-grade local NVR systems from brands like Reolink or Lorex for more expansive setups.
*   **If you need a camera for a Small Business with critical surveillance needs:** Prioritize systems with reliable continuous recording, ample local storage (NVR systems are often best), and robust notification reliability. Brands like UniFi Protect or Lorex, which offer more scalable and professional-grade local solutions, might be more suitable than consumer-grade options.
*   **If you are in an area with unreliable Wi-Fi or no internet access:** Focus on standalone cameras that record directly to a microSD card and do not rely on cloud services for core functionality. Some cellular-enabled cameras exist, but for purely local, no-Wi-Fi scenarios, options are limited and often require manual footage retrieval.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why are my Eufy notifications delayed?

Eufy notifications can be delayed due to several factors, including recent firmware bugs on HomeBase devices or cameras, issues with the Eufy Security app's background processes on your phone (e.g., battery optimization settings), or network connectivity problems between your camera, HomeBase, and router. Server-side glitches from Eufy can also cause widespread delays.

### How do I fix Eufy push notification issues?

To fix Eufy [push notification](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-security-notification-failure-june-2026) issues, first check your phone's system settings to ensure the Eufy app has unrestricted background data and notification permissions. Then, verify in-app settings for [motion detection](https://quvii.com/blog/ring-camera-motion-streaming-issues-july-2026) and notification rules for each camera and security mode. Power cycling your cameras, HomeBase, and router can also resolve temporary glitches.

### Do Eufy cameras require a subscription?

No, Eufy cameras do not strictly require a subscription for most core functionalities, especially if paired with a HomeBase for local video storage. However, Eufy does offer optional cloud storage plans for those who desire off-site backup or extended video history, which come with a recurring fee.

### What is the latest firmware for Eufy HomeBase 3 in July 2026?

As of July 2026, user reports indicate that HomeBase 3 firmware versions 3.8.2.0 and 3.8.2.8 were recently rolled out, with some users experiencing issues related to these updates. It is always recommended to check the Eufy Security app's "About Device" section for your specific HomeBase to see the absolute latest available firmware version.

### Are Eufy cameras truly private with local storage?

Eufy emphasizes local storage and on-device AI processing to enhance privacy. However, past incidents have raised questions about data handling and encryption, particularly regarding temporary cloud uploads for [push notifications](https://quvii.com/blog/eufy-push-notifications-broken-haos-2026-6-4). While Eufy states that footage is encrypted and stored locally on the HomeBase, users should review Eufy's current privacy policy for the most up-to-date information on data practices.
