## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I really need a subscription for person detection in 2026?
No. Most major brands like Eufy, Reolink, and Tapo now perform person, pet, and vehicle detection directly on the camera's hardware or a local hub. This "Local AI" is often faster than cloud-based detection because the video does not need to be uploaded to a server before the analysis occurs.

### Can I use a regular microSD card for 4K recording?
While a regular card will work initially, it is not recommended. 4K video generates a high volume of data writes, which can cause standard cards to fail within months. You should look for "High Endurance" cards (rated V30 or higher) which are specifically designed for the constant overwrite cycles of security cameras.

### Does Matter 1.4 mean I can see my Eufy cameras in the Ring app?
No. Matter 1.4 allows cameras to be viewed in "Controller" apps like Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. It does not necessarily allow one brand's proprietary app to view another brand's camera. However, it means you can create a single dashboard for all your cameras regardless of the manufacturer.

### Is 4K resolution actually better than 2K for home security?
Yes, but primarily for "digital zoom." While a 2K image looks sharp on a phone screen, a 4K image allows you to zoom in on a license plate or a face at a distance with much greater clarity. If your camera is covering a large driveway or yard, 4K is highly recommended.
